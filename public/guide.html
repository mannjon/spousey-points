Spousey Points

A little thank-you system for the people you love. When somebody does something for you, you give them points. It keeps score of kindness — no money involved.

Getting started

https://spousey-points.vercel.app

Open that link on your phone or computer. Pick a screen name and a password, and answer a few short questions. The questions simply set up the version of the app that fits you.

The first time you sign in, the app asks about invitations. If family invited you, use the screen name they gave you and you'll join their household. If you're starting fresh, you'll get a household of your own.

Tip: you can add the app to your home screen from your browser menu, so it opens like a regular app.

Points and tokens

  • Points are for giving. You get 100 points every week, added on Saturday. Spend them to thank someone in your household for something they did.
  • Points you don't spend by the end of the month go away, so there's no reason to hoard them — thank somebody.
  • Tokens are what you earn. When someone spends a point on you, you get a token. One point becomes one token.
  • Tokens never expire. Save them up for rewards. You can also trade tokens back into points, one for one, if you're short.

The four tabs

Balances — your points and tokens, and the household's children if you have any. This is also where your history and totals live.

Spend — thank somebody. Pick who, set how many points with the number picker, say what it's for, and slide to send it. There's a helper that suggests a fair amount based on how long the favor takes and how much free time you have in a week. Heavy or unpleasant jobs count extra — the app suggests it and you can flip it off.

Love Tap — the quick one, inside Spend. One tap sends a point to your favorite person, and they get a token. It's how a token changes hands — the only way.

Bounties — a corkboard of jobs. Post something you'd like done. The other person approves it (that just means "yes, that's a real job"), then anybody — including you — can pick it up. Finishing a bounty earns tokens, paid by nobody; the board just awards them.

Rewards — what your tokens buy. Each person's list is their own: you see your rewards and your children's, never another adult's. Tap a coin to cash a reward in. Your partner approves it, you tap to finish, and only then do your tokens come out.

Special Offers

At the top of your Rewards is a gold drop-down called Special Offers. These are one-time presents stocked just for you. Tap the bar to open it, tap a present, confirm — and it's yours right away. No asking, no waiting.

Kids (Family Points)

  • Children get tokens only — no points. Their tokens arrive once a week, like an allowance.
  • Parents can add or take away a child's tokens, one tap at a time, right on the child's balance.
  • When a child picks a reward from their list, it just sends the parents a note. Kids never need an approval step.

Good to know

  • Your balances are yours. Another adult in the household sees your name, never your numbers.
  • Everything is saved as you go. Sign in on any device with your screen name and password and it's all there.
  • Settings live behind the little gear near your name — that's where you can change your version of the app and your children's weekly tokens.
